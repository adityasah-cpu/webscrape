🎯 Job Portal User Guide

Complete Step-by-Step Instructions for Finding Your Dream Job

Welcome to Our Job Portal! 👋

This guide will walk you through every step of using our job portal, from signing in to applying for jobs. Follow each step carefully to get the best results!

1

Sign In with Google

Start your journey by signing in to the job portal using your college email address.
📧
Click the "Sign in with Google" button
🔑
Use your college email (e.g., your@cutm.ac.in)
✓
Verify the Google login prompt and authenticate

💡 Pro Tips

  • Make sure you're using your college email address
  • Allow the portal to access your Google profile information
  • Your profile data will be securely stored
2

Choose Your Role

Select the role that best describes what you're looking for on our platform.
👤
Select your desired role or position type
✓
Confirm your selection and proceed

💡 Pro Tips

  • Choose a role that matches your skills and interests
  • You can change this later if needed
3

Upload CV (Optional) or Skip

You now have a choice: upload your resume for intelligent job matching, or skip to browse jobs manually.

⚡ Two Paths to Choose From:

✓ OPTION A: Upload Your CV

Upload your resume/CV file (PDF or DOC format)

📄
Click "Upload CV" and select your resume file
🔍
Our system analyzes your CV and skills
🎯
You'll see matched jobs based on your resume

✓ Best Option: Get personalized job matches!

× OPTION B: Skip (Browse Manually)

Skip CV upload and browse jobs yourself

⏭️
Click "Skip" to go to main job listing
📋
You'll see all available jobs

⚠️ You can upload CV later, but need to fetch jobs first

4

Main Screen - Browse & Fetch Jobs

You're now on the main job listing page. Here you can select jobs to explore.
☑️
Use the checkbox next to job titles to select jobs you're interested in
📊
Click "Fetch Selected Jobs" or "Load Jobs" button to load your selections
📖
View the job details, description, and requirements

❓ Do You Have a CV Yet?

If NO CV yet:

① First: Fetch/select jobs from the list
② Then: Upload your CV
③ Finally: Fetch related jobs based on your CV

If YES CV uploaded:

→ You already see matched jobs (skip ahead to job details)

5

Upload CV (If Not Done Yet)

If you skipped CV upload initially, you can upload it now from the main page. Important: You must fetch jobs BEFORE uploading CV.
1️⃣
First: Make sure you've fetched some jobs (checkbox → Load button)
2️⃣
Then: Click the "Upload CV" or "Add Resume" button
3️⃣
Select your resume file (PDF, DOC, DOCX format)
4️⃣
Finally: Fetch jobs again to see CV-matched recommendations

💡 Important Notes

  • CV must be uploaded AFTER fetching initial jobs
  • Upload only PDF or Word documents
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB
  • After upload, fetch jobs again for better matches
6

Read Job Details & Description

Click on any job to view its complete details, requirements, and description.
🔎
Click on a job title to expand and view full details
📋
Read the job description, requirements, and responsibilities
💼
Review qualifications and skills needed
🔗
Look for the "Apply Now" or "Direct Apply" button

💡 What to Look For

  • Job title and company name
  • Key responsibilities and day-to-day tasks
  • Required qualifications and experience
  • Salary range (if provided)
  • Application deadline
7

Apply for the Job

When you find a job you're interested in, apply directly through our platform.
🎯
Click the "Apply Now" button in the job details
🌐
You'll be redirected to the company's application page
✅
Complete the application form on their website
✓
Submit your application

💡 Application Tips

  • Read the job requirements carefully before applying
  • Make sure your CV is up-to-date
  • Complete all required fields in the application
  • Double-check spelling and contact information
  • Apply early - some positions fill up quickly!
  • Keep track of companies you've applied to
📊

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I upload my CV after skipping?
A: Yes! But you must fetch jobs first. Go to main screen → fetch jobs → then upload CV → fetch jobs again.
Q: What file formats are accepted for CV?
A: PDF, DOC, and DOCX files. Maximum file size is 5 MB.
Q: Will my CV data be safe?
A: Yes! Your CV is securely stored and only used for job matching purposes.
Q: Can I update my CV?
A: Yes! You can upload a new CV anytime. It will replace the old one and show updated job matches.
Q: Where does "Apply Now" take me?
A: It redirects you to the company's official application page/career website.
Q: Can I apply to multiple jobs?
A: Absolutely! Browse through our listings and apply to as many jobs as you want.
