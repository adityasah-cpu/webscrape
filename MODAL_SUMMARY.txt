                          🚀 HOW TO USE IT

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: Open Job Portal
  → http://localhost:5000

Step 2: Fetch Jobs
  → Select sources (e.g., Remotive, Himalayas)
  → Click "Fetch Jobs"
  → Wait for jobs to load

Step 3: Browse Jobs
  → Jobs appear in main area
  → Each job shows: Title, Company, Type, Location, etc.

Step 4: View Job Details
  Option A: Click "View Details" button
  Option B: Click anywhere on the job card
  → Modal popup opens with full details

Step 5: Review Information
  → Read job title, company, location, salary
  → Scroll down to read full job description
  → Check all relevant details

Step 6: Apply or Close
  Option A: Click "Apply Now on Official Site"
           → Opens official job page in new tab
           → Modal stays open
           → You can continue browsing

  Option B: Click "Close" button
           → Modal closes
           → Back to job list
